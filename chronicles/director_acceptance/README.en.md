# Arrival at the refuge

Open **Help → Acceptance chronicle: the refuge**. Save your copy and press F5. Start a new game: this chronicle has its own save contract.

1. Find the club exit labelled **Refuge** and click it. Walking past is insufficient. The compatible original object is hidden to clear the opening; fixed geometry is not animated.
2. Elena welcomes you. Watch the arrival or skip it. You can speak to her directly if you interrupt the opening.
3. The guard looks, gestures and walks during the film. The camera follows a path authored in RCM, a positioned sound plays and a fade ends the film.
4. When control returns, defeat the weak rat and speak to Elena again.
5. Complete the task and travel to the second scene. Check that the reward is not repeated.

In the editor, open Campaign → Sequences → **The keeper checks the refuge**. The timed list uses normal Actions; the camera path button opens its points. The resource is under Custom resources → Project camera paths. Line-start actions are in the selected dialogue line's Properties.

This is a pending acceptance scenario. Coordinates were checked against original mesh surfaces, and output is generated and compiled. This does not certify native navigation, framing, light, audio or loading. Follow EMB01–24 in docs/MANUAL_RUNTIME_CHECKLIST.en.md. Redemption has not been launched automatically.
