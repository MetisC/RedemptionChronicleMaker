# Dynamic Encounter Lab

Talk to Anezka and choose the wave test. Then approach the barrel: entering its zone starts combat. There are two waves, a boss at 50%/25%, reinforcements and continuous spawning you can start, stop, pause or resume through Anezka. Every enemy uses the weak original Rat; only the example boss has increased health.

Save/load mid-test and check actors, thresholds and rewards are not duplicated. Use + Dynamic encounter… or the 3D ground context menu to author your own.

MANUAL RUNTIME TEST REQUIRED. See docs/DYNAMIC_ENCOUNTERS.en.md and DYN01–12.
