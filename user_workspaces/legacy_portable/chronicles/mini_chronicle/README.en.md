# The voices of London — mini chronicle

Start a new game in **Las voces de Londres · Mini crónica**. Its Spanish story remains unchanged when the editor is in English. EastLondon and GunHaven support three scenes: quay, archive and a return layout on the quay.

1. [Victoria](rcm://quay/actors/victoria), opposite the start: accept the task or refuse and speak again. She remembers the agreement.
2. The [guide](rcm://quay/actors/guide), on the left: travel to the archive. Combat is separate and optional, with one basic rat.
3. The [archivist](rcm://archive/actors/archivist): negotiate or intimidate. Receive a book and key once; your choice affects the ending.
4. The [keeper](rcm://archive/actors/keeper): return when carrying both items. Her cinema option starts seven seconds with three shots, movement and positioned thunder, then returns control.
5. The [witness](rcm://return/actors/witness): deliver the book, finish two quests and receive the reward once. The messenger returns you to the beginning to test re-entry.

## Explore and edit

**Campaign / Quests** selects scenes, quests, encounters and cameras. **+ Scene** creates layouts, including shared-map layouts. The [archive sequence](rcm://archive/sequences/two_shot) and [keeper path](rcm://archive/paths/keeper_walk) combine movement/shot changes. The [return event](rcm://archive/events/to_return) requires both items.

**Story / Resources** edits events, flags and dialogue; **Story Graph** shows decisions; **Custom Templates / AI** shows custom resources/Java. Use the Tutorial for individual explanations; this example combines capabilities in an adventure.

## Test separately

Save/reload before and after delivery, return to the beginning, refuse/abort dialogues, check ground/collision and confirm no duplicated actors, items or rewards. Mesh checks and compiled Java do not establish native behaviour. Older saves have no migration.

Editor 0.9.13 leaves this example's native gameplay unchanged. There are no invented physical doors or newly exported camera paths; those areas remain limited.
